# Color themes

Click the small **palette icon** in the toolbar popup, the side panel’s top controls, or the profile/settings navigation. Choose a swatch to apply it immediately.

EZEapply includes eight muted dark palettes: **Midnight Teal**, **Graphite**, **Dusk Blue**, **Violet**, **Pine**, **Rosewood**, **Sandstone** and **Magenta Gray**. Magenta Gray pairs charcoal-gray surfaces with muted magenta accents, inspired by the reference ChatGPT colors. Midnight Teal is the darker default. The layout and background artwork stay familiar, with quieter surfaces and buttons.

Your choice is saved on this device and shared by open EZEapply panels, profile/settings pages, AI settings and analytics. It survives normal extension reloads and updates in the same installed folder. It does not change Chrome’s own title bar or websites’ colors. Analytics keeps its teal external-form and blue LinkedIn series colors.

The chooser supports Tab, arrow keys and Escape. If saving fails, EZEapply restores the previous theme and shows an error. Theme preferences are stored separately from profiles, answers and provider credentials.
